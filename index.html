About & interests

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Nanjing University. My research examines the relationship between agent policies and their execution environments: how coordination evolves, how useful information arrives, and how runtime constraints can be learned.

My earlier work studies knowledge-aware recommendation through graph representation learning. Across these topics, I am interested in understanding the structure behind effective learning and decision-making systems.

Research focus

  • LLM agents & multi-agent dynamics
  • Speculative & asynchronous execution
  • Harness-aligned policy learning
  • Knowledge graphs & recommendation
