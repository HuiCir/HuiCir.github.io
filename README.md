# Junze Zhu · Academic homepage

Static academic homepage served by GitHub Pages at https://huicir.github.io/.

- Profile follows the author-confirmed public Google Scholar information: Junze Zhu, Ph.D. candidate, Nanjing University.
- Published papers are checked against PMLR: ICML 2026; LoG 2024 with proceedings published in 2025.
- Perseus and HPD are under-review manuscripts. Only descriptions and figure crops are included; full manuscripts are deliberately excluded, as requested by the author.
- English is the first-visit default; Chinese is available through the language control. No external fonts, analytics or application framework.

Edit `index.html` for publication metadata/links and `content.js` for bilingual copy. Styles and interaction are in `styles.css` and `app.js`.
