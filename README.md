- Published papers are checked against PMLR: ICML 2026; LoG 2024 with proceedings published in 2025.

## Perseus · The hero’s road

`Perseus/` is a custom, map-led cinematic journey at [the project endpoint](https://huicir.github.io/Perseus/). Eight acts follow the author’s original Greek quest map: the summons, the shore, the frontier, divine aid, the sea, the reflection, the Gorgon and the temple of evidence. Natural scrolling moves one shared camera and traveler along the illustrated route; scenes have distinct compositions and lighting. An atlas provides direct chapter access.

Research opens as a native scroll dialog: method, deterministic six-step demo, all six experiment families, the finance case, myth and design philosophy, and citation. Original art remains unchanged, numeric data stay in `Perseus/data.js`, and the complete manuscript PDF and LaTeX source are excluded. Figures are enlarged on demand. English/Chinese, small-screen framing, keyboard controls, reduced motion, static scientific tables without JavaScript, and printing are supported. No account, model calls, analytics or external fonts are used.
